CAMPAIGN / 001

A LIFE PEOPLE
WISH THEY LIVED.

CAMPAIGN IDEA

Create campaigns that feel like memories from a life people wish they lived.

Young, free, well dressed, cultured and effortlessly cool.

Focus on real moments, nostalgia, mystery and belonging rather than showing wealth.

Make the clothes feel like a piece of that lifestyle.

FOOTWEAR STUDY

Leather slides. Suede sandals. Worn finishes. Everyday objects made better.

BOOT STUDY

Western influence without becoming costume.

Distressed leather, subtle hardware, strong shape.

FOUNDATION PIECES

Rugby shirts. Henleys. Sweats. Tees. Denim. Puffers.
